About the Portal

Helping young South Africans find trusted work, learning, and career development opportunities in one place.

OUR MISSION

Why We Exist

Many young people struggle to find reliable information about jobs, internships, learnerships, and bursaries. Opportunities are often scattered across many websites, and fake listings can put applicants at risk.

The Youth Opportunities Portal brings verified listings and practical career guidance together so you can spend less time searching and more time preparing strong applications.

WHO IT IS FOR

Built for Young People

  • School leavers looking for their first opportunity.
  • Students searching for bursaries, internships, or vacation work.
  • Graduates and unemployed youth ready to start a career.
  • Anyone wanting to build skills through courses and learnerships.
HOW IT WORKS

Find, Prepare, Apply

  • Find: Search and filter opportunities by category, location, and closing date.
  • Prepare: Use our career resources to improve your CV, cover letter, and interview skills.
  • Apply: Follow the official application link and confirm details with the organisation.

What We Value

The principles behind every listing and guide on the portal.

Safety First

Listings are checked against official sources, and we share guidance to help you avoid recruitment scams.

Accessibility

A simple, mobile-friendly design that works well on any device and data budget.

Clear Information

Requirements, closing dates, and documents are presented clearly before you apply.

Growth

We encourage continuous learning and career development for every young person.

Important to know

  • The portal is a directory — we do not recruit or hire on behalf of any organisation.
  • Always confirm closing dates and requirements on the official employer listing.
  • Applications are free. Never pay anyone to apply for an opportunity.
  • Spotted an expired or suspicious listing? Report it to us.
